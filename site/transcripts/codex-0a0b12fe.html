That fits the original rray’s rray_unique() design and gives matching, grouping, and ordering a consistent meaning. The original rray already has uniqueness and duplicate detection, sorting, and if-else, so I would build on those rather than duplicate them.

rray_in() from vec_in()

rray_group_id() from vec_group_id()
