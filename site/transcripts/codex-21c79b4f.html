We are working on simplifying the slice loop as much as we can without impacting performance much

It just seems a bit complex to me right now, especially the start bit with rewinds at the start and end of NEXT

And I’m wondering if it even really makes sense to pre compute the full location*stride offset

If we didn’t do that, i believe we could store locations in an integer vector instead, use NA_integer_ as the natural missing slot, and do location*stride on the fly into an r_ssize

That would also allow us to immediately use existing indexes that are integer vectors without an allocation!

Look at that and also see if there are just ways you’d simplify the algorithm in general that improve human readability without sacrificing performance much
