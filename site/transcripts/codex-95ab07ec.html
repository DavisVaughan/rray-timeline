Thinking

Evaluating rray_reduce naming options

rray_reduce_flat() is a better name. It pairs naturally with rray_reduce_nested() and describes the loop structure without tying the name to a specific optimization.

I would use:

  • rray_reduce_flat(): one flat pass over x, with state stored in out
  • rray_reduce_nested(): outer loop over outputs, inner loop over reduction axes

rray_reduce_linear() is another option, but “linear” could be confused with linear indexing or algebra. flat makes the contrast with nested clearer.
